Fc Barcelona History

FC Barcelona was founded on 29 November 1899 by Joan Gamper, a Swiss businessman and sportsman, together with a group of foreign and Catalan footballers.



Barcelona Popularity

Barcelona became one of the world’s most popular clubs through its Catalan identity (“Més que un club”), its attractive possession-based style shaped by Cruyff and Guardiola, the La Masia academy, and Lionel Messi’s record-breaking career. Champions League wins and El Clásico spread its fame worldwide.
